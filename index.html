What Is a PDF File?

PDF stands for Portable Document Format. It is a file format designed to preserve the layout of a document across different devices.

How Does a Website Appear on Google?

Search engines discover webpages, analyse their content and may include them in search results. Helpful content, accessible pages and good technical setup can support discoverability, but indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.
